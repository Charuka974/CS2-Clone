Growth timeline (0 to monetization)

Month 1-2: Foundation

Post 2-3 videos per week. Test different formats, thumbnails, and titles. Focus on learning what resonates. Goal: 100 subscribers.

Month 3-4: Optimization

Double down on what works. Improve production quality. Start building a content calendar. Goal: 500 subscribers, 2000 watch hours.

Month 5-6: Monetization

Reach 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours to enable ads. Apply for YouTube Partner Program. Add affiliate links in descriptions.

Month 7-12: Scale

Increase posting frequency with AI automation. Diversify income: sponsorships, digital products, memberships. Goal: 10K+ subscribers.

Content ideas (AI-friendly)

Top 10 lists

Compile rankings with AI research. Add voiceover and stock footage. Fast to produce, high engagement.

Explainer videos

Break down complex topics into simple guides. AI generates script, visuals, and narration automatically.

Story narrations

True crime, history, mystery, or motivational stories. AI writes and narrates, you add visuals.

Product reviews

Review products via AI analysis of specs and user reviews. Monetize with affiliate links.

Tutorial content

Step-by-step guides on software, skills, or processes. Screen recordings + AI voiceover.

Compilation videos

Curate and comment on trending clips. AI generates commentary and edits timing.
